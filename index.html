Visit Wendelstein

Enjoy culture and nature in this lovely town close to Nürnberg.

Top three locations to visit at Wendelstein

Jägersee

The Jägersee in Wendelstein is a small, scenic lake surrounded by nature, ideal for relaxation and outdoor activities.

Schloss Kugelhammer

Schloss Kugelhammer is a historic castle near Wendelstein, Bavaria, known for its architectural beauty and tranquil surroundings.

Schwarzachklamm

The Schwarzachklamm is a picturesque gorge located near Wendelstein, Bavaria, featuring stunning rock formations and scenic walking paths.
